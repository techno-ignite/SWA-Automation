Defect Description
Incorrect trip credit calculated for the auto-combined trip after Auto Audit



Straight Pay Labels: A, B, D, E, F, G, H, L, M, O, R, U, W, and RP

Premium Pay Labels: P, Q, and S

Double Time Pay Labels: J, Z, RZ

Class = "C" and Class = "J" will pay double time no matter what label is on the pairing

Detailed list of steps to reproduce the defect.

Perform Auto combined scenario from CSS

Make Trip as flown

Expected Results
Trip credit is should be
DP1:  9.50 TFP

DP2: 7.71+5.00 = 12.71 TFP

DP3:  6.48 TFP

Pairing Total should pay: 13.00+17.21 = 30.21 

After audit it is paying the sum of the duty periods.  A Pilot should never be paid less than what the individual pairings were paying before combining.
